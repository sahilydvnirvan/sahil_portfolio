The growth story

What I inherited.
What I built.

I took ownership of WhatsApp marketing in October 2025. The story since then has two phases: fix the engine, then stop depending on it.

Phase 1

Fix the engine

When I took over, WhatsApp was carrying the business, and it was expensive: ₹38.8L in six months at ₹294 per lead. The same customer database was being messaged again and again, campaigns were generic, and too few leads turned into policies. The job was to cut the waste without cutting the results.

  • High cost per lead
  • Over-messaged database
  • Generic campaigns
  • Weak lead-to-policy conversion
  • One channel carrying the business

Result: six months before vs six months after.

    Quick maths: cost per lead = spend ÷ leads · premium per ₹1 = premium ÷ spend.

    Phase 2

    Stop depending on it

    A cheaper WhatsApp was still one channel carrying the business. The marketing team had only two acquisition channels, WhatsApp and the organic website, and both relied on people who already knew us. I added Google Ads and Meta Ads to reach new customers actively looking for cover, taking the team from two channels to four.

    • Only two channels
    • Heavy reliance on WhatsApp
    • No paid search or social
    • Growth capped by the database

    Result: May 2026 vs August 2026, all channels.

    MetricMay 2026Aug 2026

      Monthly premium held steady at about ₹43L while the mix changed underneath it: 57% now comes from channels other than WhatsApp. Same output, far less risk.

        How I spotted a structural growth ceiling, then pitched, built and launched new performance channels to break it.

        The problem

        When I joined CoverYou, lead generation relied almost entirely on existing databases and WhatsApp campaigns.

        Repeated campaigns to the same audience wore down engagement. Growth was capped by the size of the internal database: a structural ceiling, not a creative one.

        The insight

        “We don't need another campaign variation. We need another source of demand.”

        The learning

        Search brings intent, Meta brings reach. Scale each only as fast as lead quality allows.
